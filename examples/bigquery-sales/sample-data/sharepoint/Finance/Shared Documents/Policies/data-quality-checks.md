# Sales data quality checks

Agreed with Finance, September 2026.

- A daily load must arrive by 07:00 UTC; a late load is reported to the data team, not to Finance.
- Region revenue more than 25% below its monthly target, pro-rated to the day, is flagged to the region owner.
- Orders with a missing region or a negative amount are excluded from totals and listed in the report.
- Reports go to the region owners in the Region Owners list, and to finance-reports@example.com.
