Booking

record type

The shape of the data one step hands to the next. A step that returns a Booking must fill in every required field; anything else is rejected before the next step sees it.

travelersList of textRequiredNames as written in the email
startDate & time with time zoneRequiredDeparture, check-in or pick-up, in that place's time zone
endDate & time with time zoneRequiredArrival, check-out or drop-off
destinationTextRequiredArrival airport for flights, city otherwise
addressTextOptionalStreet address, if the email gives one
